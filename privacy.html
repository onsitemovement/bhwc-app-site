Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy explains what information Becoming Her Women's Collective LLC (“we”) collects through the Becoming Her Women's Collective app and website (app.becomingherwomenscollective.com), how we use it, who can see it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and your phone number if you choose to give one.
  • Your profile (optional): a photo, a one-line description of what you do, a short bio, and links to your social media accounts or website. You choose what to add.
  • Activity in the app: the events you sign up for and attend, your membership status and dates, bingo progress, poll answers, and the messages you send in chats.
  • Photos: your profile photo, and event photos that the owner posts to the gallery.
  • Payment records: the amount, date and status of each payment. We do not see or store your card number. Payments are taken on a secure page run by Square.
  • Notifications: if you turn on notifications, a code that identifies your phone or browser so we can send alerts to it, and your choices about which alerts you want.
  • Technical records: our hosting providers keep standard logs (such as your IP address and device type) to keep the service running and secure.

Who can see your information

  • The owner of the collective can see your account details, including your email address and phone number, your membership, your activity and your payment records.
  • Other current members can see your name, and the profile details you chose to add (photo, description, bio and links). They never see your email address or phone number.
  • People who are not current members cannot see member profiles or chats.

How we use it

  • To run your account, sign you up for events, and confirm payments.
  • To let members chat and take part in community features such as groups, polls and bingo.
  • To send alerts, reminders and announcements, if you allow notifications. You can turn them off at any time on the Notifications page in the app.
  • To send account emails, such as invitations and email confirmations.
  • To answer questions and keep the app secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who else handles your information

We use these services to run the app. They process information only to provide their service to us. Your information is stored in the United States.

  • Supabase: stores account and app data, handles sign-in, and stores uploaded photos.
  • Square: processes payments. Square's own privacy policy applies to the card details you give them.
  • Our email delivery service: sends account emails on our behalf.
  • GitHub: hosts the website files.
  • Apple, Google and Microsoft push services: deliver notifications to your device, if you turn them on.
  • The collective's technology provider hosts and maintains the app on our behalf and may access information only as needed to run, secure and support it.

Messages

Messages in group chats are visible to the members of that chat and to the owner. Direct messages are visible to the people in them and to the owner, who can read all chats in the app for moderation. The all-members chat is visible to every current member.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you have an account. You can ask us to delete your account and personal information at any time by emailing Elliana@becomingherwomenscollective.com from the address on your account. Messages you sent in group chats may remain in those chats without your name. Some payment records may be kept as required for accounting or by law.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email Elliana@becomingherwomenscollective.com.
