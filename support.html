Common questions

How do I join or renew my membership?

Open the app, go to Membership and follow the steps. Payment is handled on a secure page by Square. A membership lasts one year, and you can renew from the same screen.

I'm already a member but the app says I'm not.

You may have signed up with a different email address than the one we have on file. Email us from the address on your account and we'll link it to your membership.

I paid for an event but don't see my spot.

It can take a moment to appear. If it hasn't shown up after a few minutes, email us the event name and the email address on your account.

How do I get notifications on my phone?

Open the app and tap Notifications, then Turn on notifications. On an iPhone, first save the app to your Home Screen: open the site in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen, and open it from the new icon. You need iOS 16.4 or newer.

How do I delete my account?

Email us from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We'll remove your account and personal information, as described in our privacy policy.
